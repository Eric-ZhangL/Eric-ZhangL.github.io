Biography

I am a Ph.D. student and a member of the Communist Party of China. My research focuses on robot planning and control, and multi-robot scheduling. I was selected for the 2026 Young Science and Technology Talent Development Program for Doctoral Students of the China Association for Science and Technology. I have published 26 papers, including eight first-author SCI-indexed papers (four in Zone 1 and three in Zone 2). I have received the National Scholarship twice and was named a BIT Youth Role Model. I led a team to win a Gold Award in the China International College Students' Innovation Competition (formerly “Internet+”), contributed to engineering projects at Yantai Port, Baosteel Shanghai, and BGP, CNPC, and received over 50 national, provincial, and ministerial awards.

I received my B.E. and M.E. degrees from the School of Mechanical Engineering, Yanshan University, in 2020 and 2023, respectively, studying intelligent control of servo systems and hydraulic wind power systems. I am now pursuing a Ph.D. at Beijing Institute of Technology, combining planning, control, and AI for robot decision-making in real-world environments. My work focuses on multi-robot coordination and deployment, with the goal of bringing physical AI to autonomous driving and robotics at scale.

Research Interests

Autonomous navigation and control of mobile robots; multi-robot planning and scheduling; reinforcement learning for robotics
